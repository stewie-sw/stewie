STEWIE — how to plan a mission
  1. Site — pick where on the Moon to build: click the globe on the Plan tab, or choose a bundled site. (1·Site)
  2. Fleet — set the rover count and the max slope they may traverse. (3·Fleet)
  3. Orders — place cut / fill build orders on the map, plus keep-outs and precedence. (5·Plan)
  4. Solve — press Plan mission: the planner routes around hazards, balances cut↔fill mass, and schedules under the battery budget.
  5. Review — read the mission-control report (Report tab) and the metrics; check feasibility and the as-built flatness.
  6. Execute — watch the dry-run on the real terrain (Metrics → ▦ 3D) before any uplink. Live execution is gated to a live mission.
