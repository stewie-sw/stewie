The platform

One loop, from terrain to mission report

STEWIE turns real lunar terrain into a verifiable construction plan and a simulated execution, then progresses toward sensor-driven navigation. The conserved terramechanics authority does the physics; learned components only command.

01

Load terrain

Real LOLA south-pole DEM bundles (Haworth, Shackleton rim, Nobile rim) on a true-projected globe, or generated terrain calibrated to measured statistics.

02

Author goals

Place cut, fill, and structure orders with keep-outs, fleet and vehicle selection, on an ArcGIS-class planning canvas.

03

Plan

A routed, energy-aware Plan IR: feasibility-checked allocation, per-segment slip and grade energy, reserve-aware preconditions.

04

Simulate

Execution on a mass-conserving Bekker / slip / sinkage authority, rendered through a Godot sensor model with real camera, IMU, and power channels.

05

Report

A mission-control report plus a machine-consumable command tape: plan once, command many, with full provenance.

06

Navigate

The progression toward sensor-driven autonomy: terrain-shape, low-sun, and shadow-geometry cues for mapping, localization, and route safety.

Five operating modes

Each mode states its own truth boundary

The API and reports always label the active mode. Simulated truth is never presented as a live measurement.

GIS‑PLANLive

2D layered planning on the real DEM: slope, hazard, horizon-clipped shadow, and PSR rasters under a time-driven sun. Output is a routed, energy-budgeted Plan IR and a two-page report. Every figure traces to a tagged constant.

TRAINLive

Operator and director sessions over the real closed loop. The operator sees only telemetry-delivered, truth-denylisted legs under a mission link profile; the director gets full state and a seen-versus-actual debrief.

SIM‑OPERATELive

The live loop on the conserved authority: one persistent world that outlives clients, ROS2 teleop through slip-aware physics, strict canonical packets with the 8-camera rig and exact poses, bit-exact checkpoint and restore.

EVALUATELive

The honesty machinery: hash-anchored evidence, a role-isolated produce‑estimate‑evaluate split where the estimator is structurally denied truth, and gates that flip only via dated code-enforced artifacts.

OPERATEFuture

Consume real telemetry and issue commands to hardware. Unavailable until the command, timing, safety, and fault requirements pass. STEWIE makes no live-hardware claim today.

Architecture

A digital twin, built in named subsystems

Layered from contracts and the physical authority up to product and operations. The physical Moon is conserved; what we believe the surface is, is a separate, append-only world model.

DART
Decision & Autonomy

Perception, the articulation-parallax and shadow estimator spine, the closed-loop executive, and relocalization stops.

LODE
Lunar Operations

Mission and fleet planning: routed allocation, sequence optimization, energy and reserve accounting, the executable Plan IR.

LEAP
Localization & Estimation

Earthmoving and worksite: structure templates, cut and fill balancing, conserved skill environments, acceptance.

FORGE
Physics & Terramechanics

The physical authority: Bekker mobility, slip and sinkage, mass-conserving excavation, energy, thermal, and power.

Conserved physics for the dynamics, learned models only where they earn it. The mass-conserving terramechanics authority retains John McCardle's CC0 provenance; STEWIE's planner, Gymnasium, vehicle, perception, and visualization layers build on it. Every change made on the Moon is a layer in world state, never an overwrite.
