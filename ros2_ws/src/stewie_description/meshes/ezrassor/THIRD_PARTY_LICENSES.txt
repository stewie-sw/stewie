The mesh assets in this directory (base_unit.dae, wheel.dae, drum.dae, drum_arm.dae) are the
UNMODIFIED EZ-RASSOR rover model meshes, vendored verbatim from:

  EZ-RASSOR -- Florida Space Institute / UCF / NASA
  https://github.com/FlaSpaceInst/EZ-RASSOR
  packages/simulation/ezrassor_sim_description/meshes/
  Pinned at commit 0c5911b59dda4fa4439f6a96a66dab435436204e (2022-01-30).

They are used by urdf/ezrassor.urdf.xacro (visuals). In the STEWIE gz-sim8 overlay the meshes are
scaled 0.35 (matching the EZ-RASSOR macros.xacro scale), and COLLISION geometry uses primitives
(cylinder wheels/drums, box body) rather than the mesh, for headless physics robustness.
