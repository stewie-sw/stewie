**Producer node:** `EXP-020`
**Consumers:** `REV-010`, `DISS-010`

The independent unit is a synthetic terrain window. Action kind, eight development seeds, and system
method are repeated measures within each of eight windows. The retained report uses 4,000 paired
cluster-bootstrap draws over terrain windows with bootstrap seed `1729`.

Only two contrasts are currently computed:

- `STEWIE − latest_overwrite` harmful-plan rate: `−0.667`, descriptive 95% interval
  `[-0.667, -0.667]`;
- `STEWIE − no_physics`: `0.000`, interval `[0.000, 0.000]`.

The degenerate first interval reflects identical constructed effects in all eight synthetic windows.
It must not be interpreted as evidence of low natural-terrain uncertainty. No multiplicity-adjusted
H1–H7 confirmatory analysis was run, no pilot calibrated the minimum effects or sample size, and the
original evaluation allocation has been retired after development exposure.

Four pairs of generated spatial envelopes overlap. The intervals therefore describe this generator
only and do not justify terrain-, mission-, or planetary-population inference.
