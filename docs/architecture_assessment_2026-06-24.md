## Architecture summary

STEWIE (planner/twin/cockpit at /mnt/projects/stewie/code, HEAD 96a72fb) is a mature, honestly-tagged mission-planning platform with a load-bearing conserved-physics core, but its ARGUS dissertation track has zero closed perception-to-pose evidence: every fused-SLAM number is computed from ground-truth-plus-Gaussian-noise (dart/integrated_slam.py:73,80,88), the #1 novelty (shadow-height) is a recorded honest negative on the project's own renders (dart/shadow_height.py:11-19), and the only real-data result is 3.35 m dead-reckoning ATE over 92.5 m scored against a 46 m sanity band (test_katwijk_baseline.py). The Phase-1 intern-beta gate is integration-blocked, not design-complete: pychrono does not import (NumPy ABI break), the Chrono producer is a PLACEHOLDER_DENSITY stub hardcoding John's conda path, and the highest-blast drive-loop seam disagrees on units (commands_from_plan cell_m=5.0 vs ros2_bridge cell_m=1.0, with pose_to_odom emitting cells under a 'map'/metre frame_id) — a 5x mislocalization on the Day-28 loop. The path to a deployable platform and a defensible proposal is the same move repeated: close at least one perception-to-pose path on real truth-bearing data and pin the seams/figures so the honest results stop rotting silently. The single dominant theme is that cockpit visibility and contract-validity are being mistaken for operational and physical reality.
